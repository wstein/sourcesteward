# SourceSteward Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
Everyone participating in SourceSteward deserves a welcoming, respectful environment,
regardless of background, identity, experience or whether they contribute using AI tools.

## Expected behavior
Be considerate, give actionable feedback, respect differing views, credit contributions,
and protect private information. Critique ideas and code rather than people. Clearly
identify generated work and its verification when that matters to review.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual misconduct, deliberate intimidation,
doxxing and repeated disruptive conduct are unacceptable. Do not publish private messages
or personal information without consent.

## Scope and enforcement
This policy covers project issues, discussions, reviews and other project spaces. The
maintainer, @wstein, may edit/remove content, warn participants, restrict participation or
ban users based on severity and repetition. Enforcement should be proportionate and
consistent; good-faith reports must not invite retaliation.

## Reporting
Use GitHub's private Report abuse flow for threatening, harassing or abusive content:
https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/github-community-guidelines
For project moderation, contact the owner through available contact methods on
https://github.com/wstein. Do not post sensitive conduct reports in public issues.
If no private owner contact is available, use GitHub's abuse-reporting channel.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), a separate reporting process.
